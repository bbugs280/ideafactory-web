Process

Score first. Build second.

The expensive mistake is building something nobody wants. So the score comes before the code, and every product carries a date it gets reviewed again.

01

Score

Seven criteria: market need, feasibility, distribution, competition, moat, monetisation, and a real dollar band. Low scores stop here.

02

Build

Tests before features. Every version tagged, every change logged. AI agents track commits and flag risks while the code gets written.

03

Launch

Listing, story page, screenshots, and a measured download button — so traffic can actually be attributed instead of guessed at.

04

Monitor

Ratings, search impressions, and usage get reviewed on a schedule. Products that don't find traction get an honest reassessment.

About

It started with an argument about screen time.

A father worried his son was spending too much time playing games. Instead of banning them, they built something together — and kept going.

The son builds the products. The father brings strategy and product leadership. Behind them, a team of AI agents handles the research, the tracking, the monitoring, and the launch planning.

That's the whole thesis: AI as leverage, not replacement. Two people can run like a studio if the operational overhead is handled by agents that never sleep.

"We don't build fifty things and hope one sticks. We evaluate dozens, pick the strongest few, and give each one our full attention." — Father & Son
