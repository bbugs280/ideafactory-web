Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

IdeaFactory builds apps that respect your privacy. This policy explains how our Apps handle data.

On-Device Processing

Our Apps process data primarily on your device. We do not maintain servers that store your personal information, food logs, health data, or user content.

Data We Don't Collect

Our Apps do not collect, store, or transmit: personal identifiers (name, email, phone number), location data, browsing history, or device identifiers for advertising purposes.

Third-Party AI Services

Some Apps (e.g., GainCut) send data to third-party AI services for processing:

  • GainCut: Food photos are transmitted transiently to DashScope (Alibaba Cloud) for AI food analysis. Photos are not permanently stored on any server.

Apple Health Data

Apps that integrate with Apple Health (e.g., GainCut) read and write health data only with your explicit permission. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Subscriptions

Subscription payments are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We do not receive or store your payment information.

No Analytics or Tracking

Our Apps contain no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or user tracking.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights

Since we don't store your personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. All your data resides on your device and in your Apple Health account, under your control.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@ideafactory.one.
