Continuous conversation mode — just start talking. SpeakAble listens, your Hermes agent thinks, and you hear the response out loud. Like a phone call with your AI.

🔄 Grok-Style Barge-In

Interrupt anytime. If SpeakAble is speaking and you want to jump in, just tap the mic — it stops instantly and listens. Natural, like real conversation.

🔒 Self-Hosted, $0 Cost

Runs against your own Hermes or OpenAI-compatible API. LiveKit server is free (Apache 2.0). No subscriptions, no cloud lock-in. Your agent, your data.

🌐 Multi-Language

Speak in English, Cantonese, or Mandarin. Apple on-device speech recognition and synthesis — all voice processing stays on your iPhone.

📝 Transcripts & History

Every conversation is saved with full text transcripts. Switch between sessions, review past discussions, and pick up where you left off.

📊 Rich Markdown

Hermes can respond with tables, code blocks, headings, and bullet lists — all rendered beautifully in dark mode on your iPhone.

How It Works

You provide the brain (Hermes or any OpenAI-compatible agent). SpeakAble handles voice I/O on your iPhone. All connected through a free LiveKit server.

Step 1

Install LiveKit Server

One command: brew install livekit. Free, open-source, runs anywhere.

Step 2

Run the Bridge

Thin Python proxy connects LiveKit to your Hermes API. 4 dependencies, zero config.

Step 3

Connect SpeakAble

Enter your server address once in Settings. SpeakAble auto-derives LiveKit and token endpoints.

Step 4

Start Talking

Tap the mic, speak naturally. Your Hermes agent responds out loud. No typing, no screens.
