❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does PawMind work? Point your camera at your dog. PawMind detects their pose and posture on-device, then uses AI to generate a playful interpretation of what your dog might be thinking.
  • Is my data private? Yes. Dog photos are processed transiently by our AI provider (DashScope) and are not permanently stored. No account required. See the Privacy Policy.
  • How accurate is it? PawMind is for fun and entertainment — it's a playful interpretation of body language, not a medical or behavioral diagnosis.
  • How does the subscription work? You get 5 free scans. After that, unlimited scans require a monthly auto-renewing subscription, managed by Apple through your Apple ID.
  • How do I cancel? Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and select PawMind. Cancelling stops the next renewal.
