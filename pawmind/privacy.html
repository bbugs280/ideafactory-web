PawMind — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

What PawMind Does

PawMind reads your dog's body language and turns it into a playful, personality-rich interpretation of what your dog might be thinking. You point your camera at your dog, and PawMind detects their pose and posture on-device, then generates a short, funny "thought" from the dog's perspective.

Camera & Photos

PawMind uses your camera to capture a photo or video frame of your dog. This is the core of the app. Access is granted only with your explicit permission, and you can revoke it at any time in Settings > Privacy > Camera.

AI-Powered Analysis & Third-Party Data Sharing

To interpret your dog's body language, PawMind uses Alibaba DashScope (Qwen-VL), a third-party AI service. When you capture a scan:

  • What is sent: The photo of your dog (compressed) is transmitted to the DashScope API for image analysis.
  • Who receives it: Alibaba Cloud (DashScope), a Beijing MaaS workspace located in China.
  • Purpose: The image is analyzed to detect the dog's posture and generate a playful interpretation of its body language.
  • Data retention: Photos are processed transiently for analysis and are not permanently stored by the AI service. The resulting text ("the dog's thought") is kept locally on your device.

No personal data — no names, email addresses, account identifiers, device IDs, or location data — is ever sent to DashScope.

On-Device Processing

PawMind performs pose and posture detection on-device first. Only the necessary image data is sent to the AI service for the final interpretation. No user data is stored on any server.

No Analytics or Tracking

PawMind contains no advertising identifiers, no analytics SDKs that track your identity, no crash-reporting services, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.

Location

PawMind does not access or collect your location.

Subscriptions

PawMind subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. PawMind does not have access to your payment information. The subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; you can manage and cancel it in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Contact
